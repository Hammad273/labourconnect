NEW LEARNING OPTION

👷 Learn alongside a crew

Beginners can learn by observing experienced teammates demonstrate everyday work practices, explain tools and share trade knowledge.

Choose a trade to preview a supervised learning request. This prototype does not arrange real visits.

Safety first: Any real visit must be pre-approved and supervised by the site manager, include a safety induction and required protective equipment, and remain observation-only. No operating tools or entering restricted work zones. Adult learners only.

Prototype learning catalogue. Training availability and certification are not provided by this demo.

09⚖️

Fair Work Transparency

Break down an example job budget so the agreed daily wage is visible.

No hidden deductions are assumed. Enter sample values to see the breakdown.

11📄

Digital Work Agreement

Prepare a written record of the job terms for both parties to review before work starts.

Complete the fields to prepare a sample agreement. No agreement is signed or stored by this demo.

Important: This is a prototype template, not legal advice or a lawyer-approved contract. It does not verify identities, capture legally authenticated signatures, or guarantee recovery of unpaid wages. Any real agreement should be reviewed for applicable Indian law. A worker may keep the document and seek advice from the appropriate labour authority or a qualified lawyer about unpaid wages or unlawful deductions. No term here removes statutory rights.

10✨

Matches are based on the six sample job cards above, not real worker profiles or AI.

Prototype notice: All tools use sample information in this browser. There is no live worker verification, GPS tracking, voice server, job application, payment processing, legally authenticated contract signing, official training certification or emergency reporting. Do not enter private personal information.

OUR PURPOSE

Making work connections easier.

LabourConnect is a project concept designed to bring workers and contractors together in one place. The goal is to make opportunities easier to discover and hiring easier to organise.
